        Step 5 · Safety (5 min)

        Use AI safely at your job

        Building with AI is easy. Building safely is what makes you trusted at work.

        Quick check: AI scams

        AI can copy a voice from about 3 seconds of audio. If "your boss" calls or texts asking for gift cards, money or a password, hang up and call back on a number you already know.
