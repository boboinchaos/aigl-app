Our Story

Built on service.
Poured with pride.

The Wine Bunker was founded by Chris and Dave, two friends whose bond was forged serving our country together.

They came home wanting a place that felt like that: honest, welcoming and built around the stories people share across a table. So they built a bunker. Not to hide from the world, but to gather in it.

Wine with a PurposeAbout two dozen pours by the glass or bottle, from Napa and Willamette to the Loire and Rioja.
WhiskeyA curated shelf of bourbon and scotch, and the Smoked Old Fashioned everyone talks about.
Service & SacrificeCamaraderie, local live music and small plates made for sharing.
